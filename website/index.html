Zen Mode

It doesn't ask for a desktop.

Most chat apps want a whole monitor to themselves. Overzen shrinks down to a small, borderless pane that stays on top of every other window — your editor, your terminal, your browser — and drags anywhere you put it.

Slide the opacity down until it's barely there, or bring it back to full strength when you need to read a long reply. One click returns it to a normal window.

Built to stay out of the way

Three quiet decisions

Any model, one window

Point it at Ollama, LM Studio, llama.cpp, or Jan running on your machine — or hand it an OpenAI-compatible base URL, model name, and key for a cloud provider instead. Switch sources from the same settings panel, anytime.

Nothing leaves without asking

Local models never touch the network. If you do add a cloud key, it's encrypted at rest with your operating system's own keychain — Overzen only ever remembers whether a key is saved, never the key itself.

A PIN, not a password

Turn on App Lock and a four-digit PIN keeps the window closed to anyone else at your desk. The PIN is hashed and salted before it's stored — Overzen couldn't show it back to you even if it wanted to.

Before you ask

Questions, answered

Does Overzen send my data anywhere?

Not by default. Local models run entirely through Ollama, LM Studio, llama.cpp, or Jan on your own machine, and nothing leaves it. If you switch to a cloud provider, only the messages you send go out — straight to that provider's API, using your own key.

Is Overzen free?

Yes. It's MIT licensed and free to download and use — no account, subscription, or sign-up required.

Do I need Ollama to use it?

No. Ollama is just the default — you can point Overzen at LM Studio, llama.cpp Server, Jan, or a custom local runner instead, or skip local models entirely and use a cloud API.

What happens to my cloud API key?

It's encrypted with your operating system's own credential storage before it ever touches disk. The app only ever knows whether a key is saved — it can't display the key back to you, and your prompts are proxied out to the provider without the key ever reaching the visible window.

Is my chat history stored anywhere online?

No. Conversations are saved locally on your device only, so they're there the next time you open the app and nowhere else.

What is Zen Mode?

A one-click way to shrink the window down to a small, borderless, always-on-top pane you can drag anywhere and fade with the transparency slider — for glancing at a reply without covering what you're working on.

Can I lock the app with a PIN?

Yes. App Lock asks for a 4-digit PIN before the window is usable. The PIN itself is never stored — only a salted hash of it — so it can be checked but never recovered or displayed.

What platforms does Overzen support?

Windows today, packaged as a standard installer. macOS and Linux builds are on the way.
